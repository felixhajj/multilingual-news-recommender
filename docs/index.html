Meaning drives retrieval; optional filters add a separate matching signal. Open a result to inspect both.

QLoRA adapts Qwen for extraction. The linker is a trained classifier. E5 scores your interest against article text, not individual matched words. Phrase comparisons in the demo are illustrative probes, not proof of the model's internal reasoning.

Built and tested

From a real corpus to a working release.

5,000Historical articles collected
300Articles analyzed and indexed
2Languages: English and Arabic

Experimental prototype, not production-ready. Extraction scored 0.313 entity F1 on 20 test articles; recommendation evaluation used only two small test pools. These measured limitations, not training duration alone, limit reliability. Collecting more articles does not automatically improve model quality.

Training was a small-scale experiment, developed over hours of model-processing jobs. The selected Qwen adapter used 30 machine-assisted examples and 48 optimizer updates. The experiment history includes training, failed starts and checkpoint recovery; an exact training-only total was not fully recorded. E5 was used pretrained, not retrained.
